Evergreen Lawn Care LLC: Terms of Service for the Year-Round Lawn Plan

Pricing is per yard. Plans from $169/mo. Your exact price is shown at sign-up.

No cancellation penalty. Plans are month to month. Cancel anytime by call or text at (352) 462-0627; service runs to the end of the month you paid for, and you are never charged a fee for cancelling.

Existing customers on per-visit service (not on a monthly plan)

If you are on per-visit service and have not signed up for a monthly plan, you keep your current terms. The monthly plan terms on this page apply only to customers who sign up for a monthly plan.

Your current per-visit billing terms:

  • Your payment method is saved securely through Stripe at booking. You are charged after each completed visit, not in advance.
  • One-time services may be canceled up to 24 hours before the scheduled visit at no charge.

Monthly plan terms (Year-Round Lawn Plan)

1. Agreement

These terms apply when you sign up for the Year-Round Lawn Plan ("the plan") with Evergreen Lawn Care LLC ("Evergreen", "we", "us"), online, by phone, by text or in person. You accept them by ticking "I agree" at sign-up or by telling us yes on a recorded call or in writing. We keep a copy of the terms you accepted, with your price, on your account.

2. What the plan includes

Every visit includes mowing, string trimming and blowing clippings off the driveway, walkways and porch. The plan does not include hedges, weeding, fertilizer, leaf cleanup or other yard work.

3. Visits

Weekly from April through October and every 2 weeks from November through March, about 41 visits a year, on your street's service day. There is no winter pause. The monthly price is the same every month of the year, so in the weekly months you receive more visits than in the winter months for the same price.

4. Service area

New plans are offered in Citrus Springs (ZIP 34433 and 34434), Beverly Hills (ZIP 34464 and 34465) and Hernando, including Citrus Hills (ZIP 34442), Florida.

5. Your price

(a) Your monthly price is set for your lawn from the county parcel record: the lot size, minus an estimate of the area under buildings, adjusted for your neighborhood. Plans from $169/mo. Your price is shown before you sign up and is stated in these terms as accepted by you.

(b) We cannot price some properties from the record (for example, lots over 1 acre, condominiums, or addresses we cannot match exactly to one parcel). For those we come out and give you a free quote in person. The price you accept is the price you pay.

(c) Price lock: the first price we quote for your address stays the price for that address, even if our pricing changes later, subject only to section 6 (first-visit check) and section 12 (yearly price change).

6. First-visit lawn check

Your price is based on the county parcel record and confirmed by our crew at your first visit.

(a) If your lawn is smaller than the record shows, we lower your price to the price for the size our crew measured, before your first charge.

(b) If your lawn is more than 15% larger than the record shows, we do not charge you. We tell you the new monthly price in writing (text or email) before any charge. You may accept it by replying YES, or decline it by replying NO or by not answering. If you decline, your plan is cancelled at no charge.

(c) If your lawn is larger by 15% or less, your quoted price stays the same.

7. When you are charged

(a) Nothing is charged at sign-up. With autopay, we save your card through our payment processor, Stripe, and charge nothing until your first cut.

(b) Your first monthly payment is charged to your card (or, if you chose invoice billing, invoiced by email) on the day of your first cut. After that, the same amount is charged or invoiced every month on that date.

(c) Invoices are due 7 days after they are sent.

(d) We never store your full card number. Stripe stores it under its own security standards. You can update your card at any time using the link we email you on request.

8. Automatic renewal

The plan renews automatically every month at your monthly price until you cancel. There is no fixed term and no minimum number of months. With autopay your card is charged each month; with invoice billing you receive a new invoice each month.

9. Cancelling

(a) You can cancel at any time by call or text at (352) 462-0627, or by emailing evergreenlawncareflorida@gmail.com. We confirm every cancellation in writing.

(b) Cancelling stops all future monthly charges. Your service continues to the end of the month you already paid for, and then ends. There is no cancellation fee or penalty of any kind. Months already paid are not refunded, except as stated in section 14.

(c) We may offer you one alternative when you ask to cancel (for example, every-other-week service or skipping a visit). You do not have to accept it, and asking you once does not delay your cancellation.

10. Catch-up fee for an overgrown first visit

If your lawn is overgrown at the first visit (grass over about 8 inches), a one-time catch-up fee applies, starting at $49. Our crew confirms the amount at your first visit before anything is charged, and it is added to your first month only. We tell you the amount before your first charge.

11. Non-payment

If a payment fails or an invoice is not paid when due, we pause mowing until the balance is paid. We send reminders with a payment link. Your plan stays open and is never cancelled automatically for non-payment; visits restart on your usual day once the balance is paid. Visits that fall during a pause for non-payment are not made up. While a balance is unpaid we stop creating new monthly charges after 30 days; you can still cancel at any time under section 9.

12. Yearly price change

We may change the monthly price of an existing plan no more than once a year, in February, by no more than 5% or $10 a month, whichever is less. We will not change your price during your first 12 months. We give you at least 30 days' written notice (email, and text if you have agreed to texts) before the new price applies, and you may cancel before it applies.

13. Rain and weather

If rain or weather prevents a visit, we make it up on the next workable day and tell you the new day. Rain days are made up, not refunded. Visits you ask to skip are not refunded; skipping moves your schedule back one visit.

14. Our service promise

If you are not happy with a visit, contact us within 48 hours and we will come back and fix it at no charge. If our crew misses a scheduled visit, we reschedule it at no charge. If you believe our crew caused damage, contact us within 48 hours so we can look at it and make it right.

15. Referral credits

If someone you refer signs up for the plan and pays their first month, you receive a $50 credit on your next bill and they receive a $25 credit on their first month, after an owner approves it. Up to 6 referral credits a year per customer. Not available for existing customers, the same household or yourself. Credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred. We may change or end the referral program for future referrals with notice on our website.

16. Text messages

(a) We text you only if you agree. At sign-up, an optional box reads: "Text me about my service (visit reminders, schedule changes, billing). Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Consent is not required to buy." We will not send marketing texts unless you separately agree to receive them.

(b) Reply STOP (or QUIT, END, CANCEL or UNSUBSCRIBE) at any time to stop all texts; we confirm with one final message. Reply START to opt back in. Opting out of texts does not cancel your plan.

(c) If you cancel your plan by text, your plan is cancelled; texting STOP only stops texts.

17. Calls

Our phone line may be answered by an automated assistant. Calls may be recorded to help us serve you.

18. Property access

By signing up you allow our crew to enter your yard to do the work. Please keep gates unlocked or give us the code, keep pets secured on your service day, and keep toys, hoses and other items off the lawn.

19. Liability

We are not responsible for damage that existed before our visit, damage to items left on the lawn, or damage caused by hidden hazards such as rocks, stumps, holes or unmarked sprinkler heads.

20. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. Changes are posted on this page with a new date. Any change to your price or billing is made only under section 6 or section 12, with notice.

21. Contact

Evergreen Lawn Care LLC, Citrus County, Florida. Phone or text (352) 462-0627. Email evergreenlawncareflorida@gmail.com.
