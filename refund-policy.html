Refund Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

No cancellation penalty. Plans are month to month. Cancel anytime by call or text at (352) 462-0627; service runs to the end of the month you paid for, and you are never charged a fee for cancelling.

Monthly plan (Year-Round Lawn Plan)

This section applies to customers on the monthly plan. The full plan terms are in our Terms of Service.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, with no cancellation fee or penalty. Cancelling stops all future monthly charges. Your service continues to the end of the month you already paid for, and then ends.

Partial months

Monthly plan payments are not refunded for partial months. Months already paid are not refunded.

Rain and weather

If rain or weather prevents a visit, we make it up on the next workable day and tell you the new day. Rain days are made up, not refunded. Visits you ask to skip are not refunded; skipping moves your schedule back one visit.

Our service promise

If you are not happy with a visit, contact us within 48 hours and we will come back and fix it at no charge. If our crew misses a scheduled visit, we reschedule it at no charge. If you believe our crew caused damage, contact us within 48 hours so we can look at it and make it right.

Existing customers on per-visit service (not on a monthly plan)

If you are on per-visit service and have not signed up for a monthly plan, your refund policy has not changed. It is:

Our Guarantee

At Evergreen Lawn Care, we stand behind the quality of our work. If you're not satisfied with a service we've provided, we want to make it right.

One-Time Services

If you are not satisfied with the quality of a one-time service, please contact us within 24 hours of service completion. We will either re-service your lawn at no additional charge or issue a full refund at our discretion.

Recurring Plans (Bi-Weekly & Weekly)

Recurring subscriptions can be canceled at any time. Here's how refunds work for recurring plans:

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time with no cancellation fee.
  • If you cancel before your next scheduled service, no further charges will be made.
  • If a service has already been completed for the current billing period, that payment is non-refundable.
  • If you are unsatisfied with a specific visit, contact us within 24 hours and we will re-service your lawn or issue a prorated refund.

Weather-Related Cancellations

If we are unable to perform a scheduled service due to severe weather, we will reschedule at no additional cost. If rescheduling is not possible, a full refund will be issued for that visit.

How to Request a Refund

We aim to resolve all refund requests within 3-5 business days. Refunds are processed back to the original payment method used at checkout.

Chargebacks
