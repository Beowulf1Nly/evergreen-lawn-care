Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our service — pricing, scheduling, payments, and more.

Pricing & First Cut

Why is the first mow only $19? +
It's our way of letting you try us with zero risk. We show up, do our best work, and let the results speak for themselves. After your first visit, you're automatically billed your regular recurring rate — weekly or bi-weekly, depending on what you chose.
What are your regular prices after the first cut? +
Pricing is based on lot size. Weekly service starts at $39/mow and bi-weekly starts at $45/mow for a standard residential yard. Larger lots are priced accordingly. You'll see your exact quote before you book — no surprises.
Is there a sign-up fee or any hidden costs? +
None. You pay the introductory rate for your first mow, and then your regular rate on each visit after that. We don't charge sign-up fees, equipment fees, or any other hidden costs.
What's included in every visit? +
Every visit includes three things: a full mow at the right height for Florida grass, trim and edge along all fences, beds, driveways, and sidewalks, and a full blowout of all clippings from your driveway, walkways, and porch. All three on every visit — no à la carte, no add-ons.

Scheduling & Service

Do I need to be home when you mow? +
Nope. As long as we have access to your yard, you're all set. If there's a gate, leave it unlocked or include the code in your booking notes. We'll let you know when we're done.
How do I pick my service day? +
When you book, you'll choose your preferred start date from a calendar. We'll schedule your recurring visits from there. If you ever need to reschedule a specific visit, just call us at (352) 462-0627 or reach out before your service day.
What happens if it rains on my service day? +
Florida rain is part of the job — we work through light rain all the time. If conditions are unsafe or the yard is too saturated to mow properly, we'll reschedule you to the next available day and let you know ahead of time.
How often will you mow? +
We offer weekly and bi-weekly service. In Florida's growing season, weekly is usually the better choice to keep things looking sharp. You can set your frequency when you book, and change it anytime.

Billing & Cancellation

When do I get charged? +
We charge after each completed visit — not upfront. You'll be billed to the card on file within 24 hours of your service. For your first mow, you pay the $19 introductory rate. Every visit after that is billed at your regular recurring rate.
How do I pay? +
We accept all major credit and debit cards. Your card is stored securely and charged automatically after each visit. No need to be home to pay or tip in cash.
Can I cancel anytime? +
Yes — no contracts, no cancellation fees, ever. Cancel from your customer profile or call us at (352) 462-0627 and we'll take care of it immediately. We only ask that you give us a heads-up before your next scheduled visit.
What's your refund policy? +
If you're unhappy with a visit, contact us within 48 hours and we'll come back and re-do it for free. If we can't make it right, we'll refund that visit. See our full Refund Policy for details.

Service Area

Do you serve my area? +
We serve all of Citrus County, Florida — including Crystal River, Inverness, Hernando, Lecanto, Homosassa, Citrus Springs, Beverly Hills, and surrounding areas. Enter your address in the quote form and we'll confirm your service area instantly.
Do you serve commercial properties? +
Right now we focus on residential properties. If you have a commercial property or a larger project you'd like to discuss, give us a call at (352) 462-0627 and we'll see what we can do.
